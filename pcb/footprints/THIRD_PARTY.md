# Third-party material in `pcb/footprints/`

All footprints here are generated by `tools/gen_footprints.py` (MIT, this repo). Only one piece of geometry is adapted from another project's files. Everything else listed below was used as a dimensional or pinout reference only, either because of its licence or because no copying was needed.

Commits are the HEAD of a shallow clone made on 2026-11-15.
